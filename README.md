# Projects that I am proud of
One of my notable projects was a cross-platform chat replicator, a alternative path for communication between all platforms in just one called WarpChat. I developed it to help a friend stay in touch with our main WhatsApp group, because his smartphone was broken. By using Discord on his PC, he was able to communicate in our WhatsApp group by sending and receiving messages through Discord, while the messages were replicated across both platforms. I ended up leaving this project incomplete, even though I really enjoyed the experience of working on it keeping a "future" release to the public in mind.

Another project of mine is a public WhatsApp bot that offers a wide range of features for both groups and individual users, similar to what Discord bots provide. It includes group moderation tools, games, banking systems, leveling, items, accounts, and many, many more features. This is an active project, although development is very slow. I’ve been working on it since Jun 17, 2022. The project has been rebooted about five times for various reasons, and it has gone through several names over time, Vega, Scarlet, and Waltomation are just a few of them.
